Home

Welcome to Troop 52 in Palo Alto! We are a Boy Scout troop where scouts learn outdoor skills, leadership, and teamwork while having fun. From campouts and hikes to service projects, we help each other grow into responsible and confident young leaders.

Camping

On a typical camping trip, scouts work together to set up tents, cook meals, and build a safe campfire. Everyone has a job, from gathering firewood to cleaning up camp, and we learn how teamwork makes the whole trip run smoothly. Nights are often spent sharing stories by the fire and looking up at the stars.

Hiking

Hiking with Troop 52 takes us through local trails and beautiful natural areas around the Bay Area. Scouts practice map reading, trail safety, and Leave No Trace habits while exploring forests and hills. Every hike helps us build endurance, confidence, and appreciation for nature.

Service Projects

Service is an important part of scouting, and our troop gives back to the community through park cleanups, food drives, and volunteer events. Scouts learn that small actions can make a big difference for neighbors and local spaces. Working together on service projects teaches kindness, responsibility, and leadership.

Patrols

Propane Turtles

The Propane Turtles are known for their steady teamwork and creative camp cooking ideas. They make sure everyone in the patrol is included and ready for each outing. Their calm leadership helps new scouts feel welcome and supported.

Vipers

The Vipers bring lots of energy to campouts, hikes, and friendly patrol competitions. They enjoy learning advanced outdoor skills and pushing each other to improve. Their strong spirit and determination help keep the troop motivated.
